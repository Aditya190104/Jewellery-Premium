WEAR. LOVE. CARE. REPEAT.

Little rituals, lasting beauty.

Store each piece separately in a soft pouch. Put jewellery on after applying fragrances and lotions, and remove it before swimming, exercise or household cleaning.

Wipe gently with a soft, dry cloth. Because the materials in this demo catalogue are not verified, avoid soaking, abrasive cleaners or ultrasonic cleaning without guidance from the actual seller.

CLEAR FROM THE START

Delivery & returns.

How is sample shipping calculated?

Demo shipping is ₹149, or ₹0 when the discounted merchandise subtotal reaches ₹5,000. Sample catalogue prices are treated as inclusive; no additional tax is added in this demo.

Can I place a real order?

No. Checkout creates a demonstration record in your browser. There is no seller fulfilment, payment collection, dispatch or return service.

What happens to my checkout details?

Delivery details remain in memory on the checkout page and are discarded on reload. Order history saves only product choices, sample totals, a date and a demo reference.

AN HONEST DEMONSTRATION

Beautifully designed. Clearly a demo.

This is an upgraded Online Jewellery Shopping college/portfolio project. All prices, materials, sizes, offers, stock counts, ratings, reviews and bestseller labels are illustrative. Original supplied photography is used for presentation, not as evidence of certification or product specifications.

Shopping bag, wishlist, comparison and local profile preferences work in your browser. Checkout validates sample delivery details and creates a local demo record; it never processes a payment. There is no server-side account, login, registration or admin system in the supplied project.

Contact enquiries can be downloaded as a text file. Review drafts stay on this browser. Nothing is emailed, published or sent to a business.

YOUR BROWSER, YOUR CHOICES

Privacy & local data.

This version has no analytics, third-party trackers or external fonts. It uses local storage for shopping preferences, bag contents, local review drafts and demo order summaries. It stores no passwords, card numbers or checkout addresses.

Anyone using this browser profile can see the local data. This is not secure account storage. Clear it from My jewellery box whenever you want. Browser storage must be enabled for data to remain after a page reload.
